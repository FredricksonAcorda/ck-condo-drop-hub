## 3. WORK CLASSIFICATION MATRIX

To prevent misunderstandings, requests are categorized strictly under these definitions:

### Category A: Technical Bug / Defect (Warranty / Retainer Covered)
*Definition:* An existing, previously approved feature stops functioning as expected without any changes made by the client.
- **Examples:**
  - Submit button hangs or fails to write data due to server/database disconnect.
  - Staff Admin login fails on a supported browser using valid credentials.
  - Claim verification code displays an incorrect package status.
- **Cost:** Covered under 3-month warranty (free); covered under Monthly Retainer post-warranty.

### Category B: User Error & 3rd-Party Outage (Billable / Non-Warranty)
*Definition:* Issues arising from incorrect device settings, third-party provider downtime, or lost credentials.
- **Examples:**
  - Staff enters incorrect password or unassigned phone number.
  - Local internet / Wi-Fi disconnected at the lobby counter.
  - SMS notification fails due to zero balance on the SMS provider account.
  - Hardware malfunction of physical USB/Bluetooth handheld barcode scanner.
- **Cost:** **₱800.00** per investigation if developer intervention is requested.

### Category C: Scope Revisions & New Features (Billable)
*Definition:* Adding new visual layouts, changing business logic, or requesting tools that were not part of the initial reference template.
- **Examples:**
  - Requesting custom analytics reports or printable monthly receipts.
  - Changing color palette, theme branding, or component arrangements.
  - Adding automated WhatsApp or Viber messaging alongside SMS.
- **Cost:** **₱1,000.00 (Minor)** or **₱2,000.00 – ₱3,000.00 (Major)**.

## 4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & SUPPORT PROTOCOL

### 4.1 Support Window & Hours
- **Standard Support Hours:** Monday to Friday, **9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (PST)**.
- **Non-Working Hours:** Saturdays, Sundays, and Official Philippine Regular Holidays.
- *Note:* Inquiries sent outside support hours will be queued and acknowledged on the next business morning.

### 4.2 Ticket Reporting Protocol
To ensure rapid diagnosis and avoid back-and-forth guessing, all reported issues must include:
1. **Device Information:** Device type and browser (e.g., PC Windows / Chrome, iPhone / Safari).
2. **Visual Proof:** Clear screenshot or short screen recording of the error or freezing behavior.
3. **Exact Data Used:** The exact Tracking Number, Resident Name, or Action performed immediately before the error occurred.
4. *Requests submitted without basic context or screenshots will be placed on hold until details are provided.*

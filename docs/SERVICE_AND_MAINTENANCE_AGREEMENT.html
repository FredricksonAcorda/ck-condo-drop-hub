4. Support Hours & Ticket Reporting Guidelines

Official Support Window: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (PST). Inquiries or tickets sent during evenings, weekends, or national holidays will be queued and processed on the following business morning.

To enable rapid troubleshooting without guesswork, all reported issues must include:

  1. Device & Browser: Laptop/PC, iPhone, or Android (with browser version).
  2. Visual Evidence: Clear screenshot or screen video showing the exact error.
  3. Context Details: The exact Tracking Number, Resident Name, or Action performed immediately prior to the issue.

5. Payment Terms & Conditions

  • Per-Incident Tickets: Payable immediately upon verified resolution of the ticket.
